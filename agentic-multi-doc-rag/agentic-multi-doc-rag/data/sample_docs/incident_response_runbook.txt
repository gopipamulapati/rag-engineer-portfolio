Northwind Analytics — Incident Response Runbook

Severity levels. SEV1 denotes a full outage of the Query API or Dashboard
Frontend affecting all tenants, or any data loss in the Metrics Store. SEV2
denotes degraded performance (p99 latency above 2 seconds) or an outage
affecting a single tenant. SEV3 denotes a non-customer-facing issue such as
elevated error rates in internal tooling.

Detection. Alerts are generated by Prometheus based on Query API error rate,
Stream Processor consumer lag, and ClickHouse replication lag. Any alert that
pages the on-call engineer is treated as at least SEV2 until triaged.

On-call rotation. On-call is a weekly rotation covering the Platform team,
handed off every Monday at 10:00 AM Pacific. The on-call engineer carries the
PagerDuty primary escalation and must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes. If
unacknowledged, PagerDuty escalates to the secondary on-call after 5 minutes,
and to the engineering manager after 15 minutes.

Response steps for a SEV1. First, the on-call engineer posts an incident
channel in Slack and declares an incident commander. Second, mitigation takes
priority over root cause analysis — this typically means rolling back the
most recent deploy, failing over to the us-west-2 standby region, or scaling
up the affected service. Third, a customer-facing status page update must be
posted within 15 minutes of a confirmed SEV1. Fourth, once mitigated, the
incident commander schedules a postmortem within two business days.

Failover procedure. Regional failover from us-east-1 to us-west-2 is
triggered manually by the incident commander, never automatically, because
automatic failover has previously caused split-brain writes to the Metrics
Store. The failover script updates DNS weighting and pauses Kafka MirrorMaker
in the affected direction. Failover typically completes within 10 minutes but
can take longer if Kafka consumer groups need to be manually rebalanced.

Postmortems. All SEV1 and SEV2 incidents require a blameless postmortem
document covering timeline, root cause, customer impact, and at least one
concrete follow-up action with an owner and due date. Postmortems are
reviewed in the weekly engineering sync and archived in the incidents
repository.
